Who writes the replies?

A language model does, working from your book — its world, its cast, the Lorebook and the scene so far. That means your writing is sent to Anthropic to be read each turn. The privacy policy says exactly what goes and what does not.

How do I stop the letters?

Open the book's back cover and turn letters off. Nothing further is sent after that.

I shared a book and want it back

Unsharing takes your copy off the shared shelf. It cannot retrieve copies people already opened — a shared book is a copy, not a window.

A character said something wrong

Flag the reply. It comes to us with the scene it happened in and whatever reason you give, and it is what we use to fix how the characters behave.

Can I sign in with Apple or Google?

Not yet. Today it is an email address and a password, or a link from a friend.

Why does it ask my age?

The app is for adults only. It asks for your date of birth once, refuses anyone under eighteen, and does not keep the date.

How do I delete everything?

"Delete my account and every book" removes your books, portraits, sign-in and account from our server.
