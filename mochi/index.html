Mochi

Tap her. Say it.

Say it, and your day changes.

Tap her face and type a sentence, or say it. She does it in your real Reminders and tells you in one line, with Undo right there.

She reads you the way you mean it. "School run 8:40" is the morning. A birthday "every year" comes back every year. When a time could go either way, the other one is a single tap.

Undo

No guilt. No red.

She asks once, gently. Then she's quiet.

Something you didn't get to isn't a red number that grows. She asks about it once: move it, leave it, or put it in the drawer, where things without a day stay out of your way and never get lost.

Finish the day and she turns the page to tomorrow.

For press

Mochi, in a paragraph

Mochi is a free iPhone app for people whose to-do list makes them feel bad. A small pixel-art companion shows today from the person's own Apple Reminders and Calendar, and changes it when they tell her in a plain sentence, like "move the vet to Friday", with Undo. No account, no overdue pile, no red, and nothing kept outside Apple's own apps. Made by Berrylime Labs, a one-person studio.
