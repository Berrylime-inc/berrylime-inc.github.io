Why she's different

Your list, without the dread.

One page, one day

Your reminders and your calendar together, in the parts of your day. Nothing lives inside Mochi — she reads the Reminders and Calendar you already have and never keeps a second copy.

She never makes you feel bad

No overdue pile. No red. No “you still haven't.” Something you didn't get to is asked about once, gently — then she's quiet. When it's all too much, ask her to walk you through it, one thing at a time.

Berry, mint, sky, lavender or peach. One tap, and her room, her Home Screen widget and her Lock Screen wallpaper all follow.

For press

Mochi, in a paragraph

Mochi is a free iPhone app for people whose to-do list makes them feel bad. A small pixel-art companion shows today from the person's own Apple Reminders and Calendar, and changes it when they tell her in a plain sentence — “move the vet to Friday” — with Undo. No account, no overdue pile, no red, and nothing kept outside Apple's own apps. Made by Berrylime Labs, a one-person studio.
