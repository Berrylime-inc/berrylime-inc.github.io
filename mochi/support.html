What leaves my phone?

Your reminders and calendar stay on your phone. The words you say to Mochi are sent to a model so they can be understood, and weather comes from Open-Meteo using the location you picked. That is all — the privacy policy says it in full.

She didn't understand what I said

She takes plain sentences about your reminders — dentist tomorrow at 3 · move the dentist to Friday · not doing the gym today · clear my afternoon · done with the vet · what's left today? If she can't place a sentence she says so and adds nothing, so nothing surprising ends up in your Reminders. Try naming the reminder the way it is written.

How do I take something off completely?

Say remove the vet thing; she offers first, then removes it, and Undo stays until you dismiss it. Or tick it done in Apple's Reminders as usual — she shows what's there.

The widget isn't updating

Widgets refresh on Apple's schedule; opening Mochi refreshes them at once. They show only the next few things by design, and never anything from past days.

Erase everything

Delete the app; your reminders and events stay in Apple's apps.
