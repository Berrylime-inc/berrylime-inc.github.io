# Mochi The Assistant — press kit

**Mochi is a free iPhone app for people whose to-do list makes them feel bad.** A small pixel-art
companion shows today from the person's own Apple Reminders and Calendar, and changes it when they
tell her in a plain sentence, like "move the vet to Friday", with Undo. No account, no overdue pile,
no red.

## What it does

- **Tell her, and it happens.** "Dentist tomorrow at 3." "Move the vet to Friday." "Clear my
  afternoon." She makes the change in the person's real Reminders and says so in one line, with
  Undo right there.
- **One page, one day.** Reminders and calendar events together, in the parts of the day. Mochi
  keeps no copy of her own: she reads the Reminders and Calendar the person already has.
- **She never makes you feel bad.** No overdue pile, no red, no streaks, no scores. Something that
  slipped is asked about once ("Dentist slipped from yesterday."), with move it, drawer, remove or
  leave it. Then she's quiet.
- **Her room, in your colour.** Berry, mint, sky, lavender or peach, and it follows the real
  weather and time of day: golden hour, rain, snow, night. Home Screen widget and Lock Screen card
  to match.
- **Reads a screenshot.** A work rota or class timetable, screenshotted, goes into the days.

## Privacy, in one line

No account, no sign-in, no ads, no tracking. Tasks and events stay in Apple's own Reminders and
Calendar. Full policy: https://berrylime-inc.github.io/mochi/privacy.html
