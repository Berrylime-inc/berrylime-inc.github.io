Daily Palate — Privacy Policy

Last updated 23 September 2026 · Berrylime Labs LLC

Daily Palate is made by Berrylime Labs. Almost everything about your food stays on your phone. This page says exactly what does not, and why.

There is no account

No sign-in, no password, no email address. We do not know who you are and have no way to find out.

What stays on your phone

Your days, your plans, your goals and your food library. All of it lives in the app on your phone and is never uploaded.

Three things leave, and only for the request they belong to

Food you typed. When you ask the app to work something out, the dish name and its ingredients — or a product's name — go to a small service we run, which passes them to Anthropic's model to be read. Your day, your goals and your library never go with them.

A photo of a plate. If you photograph food, the picture is shrunk and re-drawn on your phone first, so what leaves carries no location and none of the camera's own details. It goes the same way, for that one answer.

A barcode's digits. Scanning a product sends just the number to Open Food Facts, an open food database, to get the label's figures back. Nothing about you goes with it.

The name this install has

The app makes up a random name for itself the first time it runs and keeps it in your phone's keychain. It sends that name to our own service, and nowhere else, so we can count how much one install is using and keep the free limits fair. It is not tied to you, your phone, your Apple account or anything else, and it is not an advertising identifier.

What our service keeps

Counters. How many photos this install has sent this month, how many questions today — the numbers behind the limits and nothing else. It does not store the food you typed, the photos, or the answers. They pass through and are gone.

Two things are worth saying plainly because they are not ours to promise: Anthropic reads what is sent under their own terms, and Open Food Facts is an independent open database with its own. Our service runs on Cloudflare, which keeps its own connection logs the way any host does.

The camera

The app asks for the camera only to scan a barcode or photograph food. It never asks for your photo library, your location, your contacts or your microphone, because it does not use them.

What we do not do

No analytics. No crash reporting. No advertising. No tracking of any kind, here or across other apps and websites. The app carries no third-party code.

Deleting everything

Delete the app. Everything you planned and ate goes with it, because it was only ever on your phone. What remains anywhere else is a counter against a random name, which expires by itself.
