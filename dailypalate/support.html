Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-in and nothing to remember. Your food lives on your phone.

What happens when I ask it to work something out?

The food you typed goes to a model to be read, and the answer comes back. Your day, your goals and your library stay on the phone. The privacy policy says it in full.

Why does it want the camera?

To scan a barcode, or to photograph a plate so it can work out what is on it. It never asks for your photo library.

It says I have hit a limit

There are fair-use limits on photos each month and questions each day, so one install cannot run up the bill for everyone. They reset on their own.

A barcode came back wrong

The figures come from Open Food Facts, an open database anyone can correct. If a product is wrong there, it will be wrong here — and fixing it there fixes it for everyone.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app. It was only ever on your phone.
